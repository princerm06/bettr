# Product Rules

Rules that should remain true even when implementation details change.

- Claiming credit for meaningful real-world action should be fast and low-friction.
- Deterministic rules decide scoring/XP; ML provides semantic assistance.
- Ambiguous logs should be clarified rather than confidently misclassified.
- Users may receive category suggestions without being forced into them.
- User priorities should influence progress meaningfully without making lower-priority areas worthless.
- Zero-credit outcomes are valid when an action does not satisfy the scoring rules.
- Progressive disclosure: beginner simplicity first, advanced control later.
- Social features should support accountability and growth rather than passive consumption.
- User-entered content and history should be treated as private by default.
- New product ideas should fit the current roadmap or remain deferred rather than silently expanding scope.

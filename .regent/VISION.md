# Vision

Bettr is a private/social whole-person self-improvement and accountability app.

## North star
Help users turn meaningful real-world actions into visible progress without making self-improvement feel like paperwork.

Core loop:

priorities -> plans/actions -> semantic validation -> deterministic credit -> priority-weighted progress -> reflection/insights -> adjustment

Bettr should feel simple enough for a beginner while becoming deeper as the user engages more.

## Non-goals
- Do not turn Bettr into an infinite-scroll social network.
- Do not make AI the authority over whether a user "deserves" progress.
- Do not optimize for engagement at the expense of genuine self-improvement.
- Do not expose advanced complexity before users need it.
- Do not replace user agency with opaque automation.

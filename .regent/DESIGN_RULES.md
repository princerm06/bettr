# Design Rules

UX/UI principles Regent should preserve.

- Keep primary flows simple and understandable without documentation.
- Prefer progressive disclosure over exposing every option at once.
- Logging an action should feel faster than journaling or form-filling.
- AI/semantic feedback must be understandable and non-authoritative.
- Clearly distinguish suggestions from decisions.
- Avoid clutter, unnecessary modal chains, and duplicate controls.
- Mobile behavior is a first-class concern.
- Preserve room for multiple visual themes without coupling core behavior to one brand aesthetic.
- Social surfaces should avoid infinite-scroll or addictive interaction patterns.
